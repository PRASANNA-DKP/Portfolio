01 / ABOUT

WHY I
CHOSE
ROBOTICS?

I chose robotics because I enjoy building things that bring different areas of engineering together. From 3D design and electronics to embedded programming and robotics software, robotics gives me the opportunity to work across both hardware and software and turn an idea into a working system.

What I enjoy most is the complete process — designing the robot, integrating sensors and electronics, developing the software, testing it in simulation, and finally seeing everything work together on real hardware. My current focus is autonomous mobile robotics, where I’m working with ROS2, URDF/Xacro, ros2_control, SLAM and Nav2 while developing both simulated and physical robots.

I also enjoy experimenting with new ideas and sharing what I learn with students and fellow learners through practical projects. For me, robotics is more than just building machines — it’s about bringing different technologies together to create something that can actually sense, think and move.
